Good to know

Explanation of our services

What is a Maintenance Clean?
We come and maintain the cleanliness of your home on a weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly basis.
What is a Deep Clean?
We go a bit further than the maintenance clean and go literally above and beyond. (Ex: wash baseboards, windows, ceiling fans, internal fridge, ovens etc.)
What is Property Maintenance?
We take care of your home while you're away. Trash need to go out? Lawn needs picked up? Faucet leaking? We maintain your home.
What is Client Pet Sitting?
If you are already a client we can take care of your pet while away. We can do in-home or drop-in care. (We also take client referrals for pet sitting as well.)
What is Client House Sitting?
If you are already a client we can watch over your home while you are away — checking in and keeping things cared for until you return. (We also take client referrals for house sitting as well.)
What are Client Small Repairs?
We now offer small repair jobs for our clients, specializing in small plumbing work — leaky faucets, running toilets, and other minor fixes around the home.
What is our Laundry Service?
Wash, dry, and fold — we can take care of laundry as part of your clean or as its own service. After all, it's in our name: Dirty Beach Laundry and Cleaning!
